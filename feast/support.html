Cuetip Feast: Support

How to play

  • Cuetip swings each dish on a rope under his pumpkin balloon. Tap anywhere to drop it.
  • Stack the dishes on the cake stand as high as you can. Your score is the height of the tower.
  • A dish that slides off onto the table breaks a plate. You have three plates per feast.
  • Land three perfect drops in a row to win a plate back. The gravy boat glues the whole tower in place.

Common questions

How do I earn acorns?
Every feast earns acorns for dishes stacked, perfect drops and height. Finish the daily goals on Today's Menu for more, or watch an ad to double a feast's acorns.

What can I buy?
Open the Cupboard to buy new tablecloths and cake stands with acorns.

I broke my last plate. Now what?
You can watch an ad for a second helping (one more plate, once per feast) or start a new feast.

Does it need internet?
No. The game works offline and your progress is saved on your device. Ads need a connection.
