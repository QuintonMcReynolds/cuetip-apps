Cuetip's Hideout: Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Cuetip's Hideout is free to play and is supported by ads. CuetipLLC does not collect, store or sell your personal data. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

What the app stores

Your coins, rooms, friends, goals and settings are saved only on your own device. CuetipLLC never receives them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Amazon Appstore (Fire tablet) version

The Fire tablet version of Cuetip's Hideout is free and supported by ads from Liftoff Monetize (Vungle). CuetipLLC still does not collect or receive any of your data. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Liftoff may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address, general location, device type and how you interact with ads. You can reset your Advertising ID or turn off interest-based ads in your Fire tablet's privacy settings. Ads need an internet connection; the game itself still works offline. Liftoff's use of this data is covered by Liftoff's Privacy Policy.

Ads on iPhone (Google AdMob)

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, general location, device type and how you interact with ads. On first launch the app asks whether you allow tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown but are not personalized. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Players in the EU and UK are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No in-app purchases
  • No analytics beyond what Google AdMob collects for ads
  • The game works offline. Ads need an internet connection.

Children

Cuetip's Hideout is not directed at children under 13, and CuetipLLC does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
