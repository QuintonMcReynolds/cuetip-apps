Cuetip's Hideout: Support

How to play

  • A coin bubble over a critter means a gift. Tap it to collect coins.
  • Tap a critter without a gift to pet them: hold and rub back and forth until the heart meter fills.
  • Open the Catalog to buy furniture, rugs, wall decor, wallpaper and floors. Cozier rooms give bigger gifts.
  • Use tickets to Summon new friends. Each friend has a favorite item and moves into the room that has it.

Common questions

How do I move or rotate furniture?
In the Catalog, drag an item to move it. Tap it, then use Turn or Put away.

How do I get more summon tickets?
You get one every level-up and one for finishing all three daily goals.

How do I unlock new rooms?
Reach the level shown on the Rooms screen and build the room with coins.

Why can't I pet a friend?
Friends rest for a few minutes after petting. Collect their gifts meanwhile.

Does it need internet?
No. The game works offline and your progress is saved on your device. Ads need a connection.
