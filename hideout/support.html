Cuetip's Hideout: Support

How to play

  • Tap an item, then tap an empty spot on another shelf to move it.
  • Three matching items on one shelf clear away. Items in the back slide forward.
  • Clear every shelf to win coins and stars, then spend them in Decorate.

Common questions

I'm stuck.
Use Undo, or Shuffle to mix up the shelves.

How do I unlock friends?
New critter friends move in as you reach higher levels. Tap them in your room to say hi.

Can I move furniture?
Yes. Open Decorate, tap a placed item, then use Flip or Put away.

Does it need internet?
No. It works fully offline, and your progress is saved on your device.
