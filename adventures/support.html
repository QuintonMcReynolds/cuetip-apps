Adventures of Cuetip: Support

About the game

A story adventure with training battles. Cuetip fights a spreading curse and the Rot King Vexmoor, with friends Bramble, Dr. Moss, Kit and Ozzie at his side. Rated 18+ for mature themes.

Common questions

How do I save?
The game saves automatically on your device.

I lost a battle.
You can retry right away. Train your friends' moves (Guard, Parry, Focus, Dash, Heavy) and try a different order.

Does it need internet?
No. It works fully offline.
