Cuetip Burrows: Support

How to play

  • Place exactly one Cuetip in every row, every column and every colored burrow.
  • No two Cuetips may touch, not even diagonally.
  • Tap a square once for ✕, twice for a Cuetip. Drag to mark many ✕s.

Common questions

Is there always a solution?
Yes. Every one of the 120 puzzles has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone, no guessing.

I'm stuck.
Tap the lightbulb for a hint, or use undo and restart.

How do I reset my progress?
Home screen → Settings → Reset progress.

Does it need internet?
No. It works fully offline.
