Cuetip Burrows: Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

Cuetip Burrows does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The Fire tablet version shows ads, described below.

What the app stores

Your puzzle progress and settings are saved only on your own device. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Amazon Appstore (Fire tablet) version

The Fire tablet version of Cuetip Burrows is free and supported by ads from Liftoff Monetize (Vungle). CuetipLLC still does not collect or receive any of your data. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Liftoff may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address, general location, device type and how you interact with ads. You can reset your Advertising ID or turn off interest-based ads in your Fire tablet's privacy settings. Ads need an internet connection; the game itself still works offline. Liftoff's use of this data is covered by Liftoff's Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads in the Apple versions (see the Fire tablet section above)
  • No analytics or tracking by CuetipLLC
  • The game works fully offline.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. CuetipLLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
