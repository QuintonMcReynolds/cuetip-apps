Cuetip: Night Swarm: Support

How to play

  • Drag anywhere to move. Cuetip attacks on their own.
  • Collect gems to level up and pick a new weapon or upgrade.
  • Survive 10 minutes until sunrise. Watch out for the Abyss Warden.
  • Spend moon coins between runs on permanent upgrades.

Common questions

How do weapons evolve?
Get a weapon to level 5, own its matching passive, then open a chest (elites drop them). The level-up card tells you which passive it needs.

How do I earn moon coins?
Pick up coins during a run. You also get a bonus at the end based on time survived and kills, plus a big bonus for reaching sunrise.

Can I turn off sound or vibration?
Yes. Tap ⚙ on the title screen.

Does it need internet?
No. It works fully offline, and your progress is saved on your device.
