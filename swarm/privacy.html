Cuetip: Night Swarm: Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Cuetip: Night Swarm does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Your best times, moon coins, upgrades and settings are saved only on your own device. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No in-app purchases
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No network access. The game works fully offline.

Children

Because no data is collected, the app is safe for players of all ages.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
