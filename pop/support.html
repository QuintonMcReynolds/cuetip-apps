Cuetip Pop: Support

How to play

  • Tap a kernel to pop it. The number shows how many spicy kernels touch it, even corner to corner.
  • Long-press, or switch to Flag, to mark a kernel you think is spicy.
  • Tap a number once all its spicy neighbors are flagged to pop the rest at once.
  • Popping a spicy kernel costs a heart. Pop every safe kernel to win.

Common questions

Is my first tap safe?
Always. Spicy kernels are placed after your first tap, never on or next to it.

How do I get 3 stars?
Finish with all three hearts.

Can I turn off long-press flagging?
Yes, in Settings.

Does it need internet?
No. It works fully offline, and your progress is saved on your device.
