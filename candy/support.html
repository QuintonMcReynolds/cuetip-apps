Cuetip Candy: Support

How to play

  • Swipe a candy into its neighbor to swap. Line up 3 or more of the same to pop them.
  • 4 in a row makes a striped candy that clears a whole line.
  • An L or T shape makes a wrapped candy that blasts everything around it.
  • 5 in a row makes a choco bomb. Swap it with any candy to clear that color.
  • Swap two special candies together for a giant combo.

Common questions

What are the pink tiles?
Jelly. On jelly levels, pop candies on top of every jelly tile to win. Darker jelly takes two pops.

I can't see any moves.
Wait a few seconds and Cuetip will highlight one. If there are none, the board shuffles itself.

How do stars work?
Win the level for one star. Higher scores earn two or three. Leftover moves add bonus points.

Does it need internet?
No. It works fully offline, and your progress is saved on your device.
