Cuetip Snowball Squadron: Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Cuetip Snowball Squadron is free to play and is supported by ads. CuetipLLC does not collect, store or sell your personal data. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

What the app stores

Your best score, cookies, upgrades, sleds, daily goal progress and settings are kept only on your own device. CuetipLLC never receives them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, general location, device type and how you interact with ads. On first launch the app asks whether you allow tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown but are not personalized. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Full-screen ads only appear between flights, never while you are playing. Rewarded ads (keep flying, double cookies, free cookies) only play when you tap to watch one.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No in-app purchases
  • No analytics beyond what Google AdMob collects for ads
  • The game works offline. Ads need an internet connection.

Children

Cuetip Snowball Squadron is not directed at children under 13, and CuetipLLC does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
