Cuetip Snowball Squadron: Support

How to play

  • Drag anywhere on the screen to fly Cuetip's sled left and right. He throws snowballs all by himself.
  • Knock out grumpy clouds, robot elves, icicle bats and snow bruisers. Dodge their icicles and gumdrops.
  • You have three hearts. Every knockout without getting hit builds your streak, up to five times the points.
  • Every fifth wave the Blizzard King shows up. Beat him for a big pile of cookies and a heart.

Common questions

What do the power-ups do?
Triple throw sends three snowballs at once, the cocoa mug makes Cuetip throw twice as fast, the scarf shield blocks one hit and the heart gives a heart back.

How do I earn cookies?
Baddies drop cookies when you knock them out. Fly into them to catch them. Finish the daily goals for more, or watch an ad to double a flight's cookies.

What can I buy?
Open the Workshop to upgrade Throw Power, Throw Speed and the Cookie Magnet, or buy new sleds.

I lost my last heart. Now what?
You can watch an ad to keep flying (two hearts back, once per flight) or start a new flight.

Does it need internet?
No. The game works offline and your progress is saved on your device. Ads need a connection.
