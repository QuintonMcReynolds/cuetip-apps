Cuetip Duel: Support

How to play

  • Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left side to move. Right thumb: drag on the right side to look and aim.
  • Hold the red button to fire. With Auto-fire on (the default), your blaster also fires by itself while the crosshair is on your rival.
  • The blue button jumps; the purple button reloads.
  • Win a round by knocking out your rival, or by having more health when the timer runs out. First to 3 rounds wins the match.
  • Grab the Carrot Launcher from the center platform for 3 explosive shots, and the health packs behind the fences to heal.

Common questions

How does Quick Match work?
It pairs you with anyone searching at the same time. If nobody turns up, you can practice against a CPU rival instead (the game offers this after a few seconds and starts one automatically after 45 seconds). CPU rivals are always labeled CPU.

How do I play my friend?
Tap Play a Friend. One of you taps Open a Room and reads out the 6-letter code; the other types it in and taps Join.

What are trophies?
Win online matches to earn trophies (+30), lose to drop a few (−15). Beating the CPU gives +8. Trophies raise your rank and unlock fur colors. You can also unlock a fur color early by watching a short ad.

What if someone leaves mid-match?
The player who stays wins. Leaving a match you've started counts as a loss.

Does it need internet?
Online matches do. Practice vs CPU works offline.

The controls feel too fast or slow.
Open Settings (the gear) and change Look speed. You can also turn Auto-fire and Aim assist on or off there.
