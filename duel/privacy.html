Cuetip Duel: Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Cuetip Duel is a free online game supported by ads. CuetipLLC does not sell your personal data. To match you with other players the game uses Unity Gaming Services, and ads are provided by Google AdMob. Both collect some data, described below.

What the app stores on your device

Your generated player name, trophies, wins, fur color and settings are kept on your own device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Online matches (Unity Gaming Services)

When you play online, the app signs you in anonymously with Unity Authentication. This creates a random player ID; there is no account, email or password. Unity's matchmaking and relay services use that ID and your IP address to find a match and carry game traffic between the two phones.

During a match, your rival's device receives your generated player name, fur color, trophy count and your moves (position, aim and shots). Players cannot type names or send messages, so nothing you write is shown to other players.

Unity's handling of this data is covered by the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Practice vs CPU works offline and sends nothing.

Ads (Google AdMob)

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, general location, device type and how you interact with ads. On first launch the app asks whether you allow tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown but are not personalized. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Full-screen ads only appear after a match, never during a fight. Rewarded ads (unlocking a fur color early) only play when you tap to watch one.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, email or passwords
  • No chat or typed names
  • No in-app purchases
  • No analytics beyond what Unity Gaming Services and Google AdMob collect as described above

Children

Cuetip Duel is not directed at children under 13, and CuetipLLC does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
