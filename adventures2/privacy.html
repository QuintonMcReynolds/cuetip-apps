Adventures of Cuetip 2: The Blight Moon: Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Adventures of Cuetip 2 does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The Fire tablet version shows ads, described below.

What the app stores

Your save files, unlocked endings and settings are saved only on your own device. CuetipLLC never receives them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Amazon Appstore (Fire tablet) version

The Fire tablet version of Adventures of Cuetip 2 is free and supported by ads from Liftoff Monetize (Vungle). CuetipLLC still does not collect or receive any of your data. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Liftoff may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address, general location, device type and how you interact with ads. You can reset your Advertising ID or turn off interest-based ads in your Fire tablet's privacy settings. Ads need an internet connection; the game itself still works offline. Liftoff's use of this data is covered by Liftoff's Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads in the Apple versions (see the Fire tablet section above)
  • No in-app purchases
  • No analytics or tracking by CuetipLLC
  • The game works fully offline.

Age rating

The game is intended for adults (18+) because of its mature themes. CuetipLLC collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that collects data, this page will be updated before that version ships.
