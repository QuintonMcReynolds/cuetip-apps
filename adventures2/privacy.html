Adventures of Cuetip 2: The Blight Moon: Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Adventures of Cuetip 2 does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.

What the app stores

Your save files, unlocked endings and settings are saved only on your own device. CuetipLLC never receives them. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No in-app purchases
  • No analytics or tracking
  • The game works fully offline.

Age rating

The game is intended for adults (18+) because of its mature themes. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that collects data, this page will be updated before that version ships.
