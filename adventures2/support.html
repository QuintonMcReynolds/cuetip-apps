Adventures of Cuetip 2: The Blight Moon — Support

About the game

One year later, Lady Nightshade raises the Blight Moon. Cuetip has seven nights to stop her, with old friends and new ones: Rook the raven, Pip and Sir Quill. Rated 18+ for mature themes.

Common questions

How do I save?
The game saves automatically on your device.

What's new in battles?
Ward reflects green bolts, purple snares can't be blocked so dash through them, and the Together meter lets friends jump in to help.

Do I need to play Vol. 1 first?
It helps for the story, but Vol. 2 teaches everything you need.

Does it need internet?
No. It works fully offline.
