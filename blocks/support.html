Cuetip Blocks: Support

How to play

  • Drag a block from the tray onto Cuetip's garden. Blocks don't rotate.
  • Fill a whole row or column to clear it. Clear several at once, or clear on back-to-back moves, for bigger points.
  • Each level has goals (score, weeds, golden carrots, trapped friends, Blight goo) and a limit on pieces. Finish with more pieces left for more stars.
  • Classic is the endless mode: play until there's no room left and beat your best score.

Common questions

What do the boosters do?
Shovel digs out one block, Reroll swaps the pieces in your tray, and Hint shows a good move. You start with three of each; buy more with carrots.

How do I earn carrots?
Beat levels (more stars, more carrots), collect golden carrots in Classic, or watch an ad on the win screen to double them.

I ran out of pieces. Now what?
You can watch an ad for 3 more pieces (once per try), buy 5 more with carrots, or try again.

What are stones and goo?
Stones take two clears: the first cracks them. Blight goo grows every few moves unless you clear some of it.

Does it need internet?
No. The game works offline and your progress is saved on your device. Ads need a connection.
